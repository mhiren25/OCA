The script
0:00–0:12 · The problem, in their words

A client emails an order. Someone reads it, works out what it says, and retypes it into the order screen. Every time. This is that step.

Screen: the email, nothing else.

0:12–0:30 · The email as it really arrives

This is what one actually looks like. Forwarded twice, a signature, a disclaimer, and the instruction is three lines in the middle.

Screen: scroll the email so they see the mess.

I'm pasting it in here. In production this comes from the mailbox — that connection is the next piece of work.

Say that line and move on. Owning the gap in one sentence costs four seconds; being asked about it later costs the room.

0:30–0:52 · The capture

It's reading it now. Parsing the message, finding the part that's actually an instruction, calling the model, checking its work.

Screen: the trace filling in. Let it run — it is more interesting than a spinner and it fills the pause naturally.

Three orders, about ten seconds.

0:52–1:15 · Why it can be trusted — the two beats that matter

The first thing it does is decide what to ignore.

Screen: toggle the ignored regions. Let it sit for two seconds.

The quoted thread, the signature, the disclaimer — all discarded. It acted on nine lines out of forty-seven. It isn't reading the whole email and hoping.

Screen: hover a value so the source highlights.

And every value points back at the words that produced it. This quantity came from here. Nothing in this draft is here because the model thought it sounded right.

This view is how we check the agent's work. Whether it belongs inside the order screen is a question for you.

Say that second line. This page is a testing tool and 029 removes it in production, so demonstrating it without framing sets an expectation you would later have to withdraw. Framed as a question it becomes a product conversation instead — and if they ask for the provenance view inside Topaz, that is a much better outcome than proposing it yourself.

Keep the distinction clear while you say it: the page is the host, the hover-to-source rendering is the capability. The page will not be in production. The capability could sit in the order screen tomorrow.

1:15–1:30 · What it refuses to do

Screen: an unresolved field.

Where the client didn't say something, it says so. It doesn't guess a price and it doesn't pick an account. Across the whole test set it has never invented a value — that's measured, not asserted.

1:30–2:00 · The payoff — the order is already in Topaz

Screen: switch to the order screen. Open the order.

And it's already here. This is the same screen your traders use, with the order in it — instrument, quantity, account, all of it.

Let them look at it for a beat. This is the moment the demo exists for.
